#### How does VOTE verify my identity before I can cast a ballot?
> VOTE uses secure multi-factor authenification to verify the identity of a voter by utilizing a combination of biometrics and identity verification protocols that align with state voter registration databases

#### Is my vote anonymous and private?
> Yes. VOTE uses advanced cyptographic protocols to separate you from your ballot, ensuring ballot security and anonymity once you submit your ballot

#### Can I use this app if I am registered to vote in any state?
> Availabilty depends on your specific state's elections laws. VOTE: Mobile Voting is only available in select states at this time. Those states include Kentucky, West Virginia, and Ohio

#### What happens if I accidentally close the app while filling out my ballot?
> The app saves your progress locally on your session and can be re-opened to pick back up where you left on after completing authenification.
