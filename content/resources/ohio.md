## Voting in Ohio

Ohio residents can vote in person on Election Day, vote early, or request an absentee ballot by mail. Any registered Ohio voter can request an absentee ballot without needing a specific reason.

Ohio requires an accepted form of photo identification for in-person voting, such as an Ohio driver's license, state ID, U.S. passport, or military ID. College student IDs are not currently accepted for in-person voting.

## College Students

College students attending school outside their home state may face challenges when trying to vote. Ohio students attending college in another state can request an absentee ballot to vote from their registered Ohio address.

Students attending college in Ohio may also be eligible to register using their campus address if they meet the state's residency requirements.

## How VOTE Could Help

VOTE: Mobile Voting proposes an easier way for college students to participate in elections while attending school away from home.

Students could visit an approved campus verification center, present their student ID along with any additional required identification, and verify their voter registration.

Once verified, students could securely cast their ballot through the VOTE application for the state and district where they are registered.

Our proposal would expand identification options for students and reduce the need to travel home or mail absentee ballots.

This system would require changes to Ohio election laws, approval from election officials, and security testing before becoming an official voting option.
