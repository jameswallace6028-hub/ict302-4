## About Us

We recognized a problem where voting numbers in younger generations is lower because of voting limitions and accessibility problems. **VOTE** bridges the gap between election infrastructure and modern accessibility, letting verified voters cast their ballots safely without taking off work or standing in hours-long queues.

## How It Works

1. **Identity Verification:** Upload a state-issued ID and perform a one-time biometric liveness check.
2. **Interactive Ballot:** Select your candidates or ballot measures with real-time error prevention.
3. **Encrypted Submission:** Your vote is encrypted *on your device* before transmission, stripping all personal metadata.
4. **Receipt Generation:** Receive an anonymous receipt code to confirm your ballot was counted.

(*VOTE: Mobile Voting will never save biometric data and personal information, all information is erased after ballot casting*)
