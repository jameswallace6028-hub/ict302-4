**SamDaMod** | *Moderator* | Today, 11:42 AM
> Hey Alex! It's actually not a bug with your phone. While VOTE  lets you cast your ballot directly from your device once you're verified, the app itself doesn't handle initial voter registration. 
> 
> To use the app, you'll first need to register to vote through your state's official requirements.
> 
> Once your state processes your registration and adds you to the voter rolls, come back to the app and run the **Voter Eligibility Verification** step. It will sync with your state record and unlock your digital ballot!
