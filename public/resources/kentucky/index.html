Kentucky Voting Resources

Voting in Kentucky

Kentucky residents can vote in person on Election Day, vote early, or request an absentee ballot if eligible. Voters must be registered and provide an accepted form of identification. College-issued photo IDs are accepted for in-person voting.

College Students

Kentucky allows students attending college outside their home county or state to request absentee ballots. However, this process can be inconvenient for students balancing classes, work, and other responsibilities.

How VOTE Could Help

VOTE: Mobile Voting proposes a simpler way for college students to participate in elections. Students attending school outside their home state could visit an approved campus verification center, present their student ID and any additional required identification, and verify their voter registration.

Once verified, students could use the VOTE application to securely cast a ballot for their registered home state without traveling home.

This system is a proposed feature and would require legal approval and security testing before becoming an official voting option.
