West Virginia Voting Resources

Voting in West Virginia

West Virginia residents can vote in person on Election Day, vote early, or request an absentee ballot if eligible. The state also allows certain voters, including eligible military personnel, overseas voters, and individuals with physical disabilities, to submit ballots electronically.

College Students

West Virginia allows college students attending school outside their home county to request absentee ballots if they cannot vote in person during the available voting period.

However, students attending college in another state may find it difficult to travel home or manage absentee voting deadlines while balancing classes and work.

How VOTE Could Help

VOTE: Mobile Voting proposes a more convenient option for college students attending school away from home. Students could visit an approved campus verification center, present their student ID and any additional required identification, and verify their voter registration.

Once verified, students could securely cast their West Virginia ballot through the VOTE application without traveling home.

Since West Virginia already permits electronic ballot return for certain eligible voters, our proposal would explore expanding this technology to college students.

This expansion would require changes to election laws, approval from election officials, and additional security testing.
